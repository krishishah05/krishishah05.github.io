From watching my dad solder circuit boards to leading research, teaching, and building things that matter. Technology has always been my story.

I remember the days my father used to call me down from my room whenever he was fixing the TV. I used to watch in disbelief as my dad simply cracked open the TV, straight down the middle, and melted a piece of metal between the wires. I soon learned this process was called "soldering." This captivation struck me; I had no clue how a simple piece of metal could change the way I viewed my daily TV shows.

I further fed into this fascination through high school classes, diving into AP Computer Science Principles my freshman year. I loved playing probability card games with my parents, so I built my first statistics-based online application: 7 Up 7 Down! And that's where it all started.

Fast forward: I've built PriceAlert (real-time stock alerts), RoboRX (an autonomous medication-delivery robot), and a Google Search Query Recommendation System using BERT and Random Forest. I got a sense of thrill throughout every step of solution-creating, app-building, and shipping new software for better functionality.

As an undergraduate student at NJIT's Albert Dorman Honors College, I am excited to see what my future has in store, making new memories, taking in immeasurable sums of knowledge, and navigating through any obstacles that come my way. Each individual has their own journey, their own path, and their own dream. This one is mine.
